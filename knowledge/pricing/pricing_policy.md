# Enterprise Pricing Policy & Margin Governance

## 1. Executive Overview
PriceMind AI enforces corporate pricing policies to maximize sustainable gross margin while protecting market share and customer goodwill. All algorithmic price recommendations generated by the dynamic pricing engine must strictly adhere to the guardrails, approval tiers, and cadence limits defined in this policy document.

## 2. Category Margin Floors and Guardrails
Every product category operates with mandatory minimum gross margin floors. Under no circumstances may an automated pricing recommendation set a price yielding a margin below these absolute thresholds:

- **Enterprise Hardware (Electronics)**: Absolute margin floor is 25.0%. Recommended target margin range is 38.0% to 55.0%.
- **High-Precision Sensors**: Absolute margin floor is 30.0%. Recommended target margin range is 45.0% to 65.0%.
- **Industrial Networking**: Absolute margin floor is 28.0%. Recommended target margin range is 40.0% to 58.0%.
- **Consumables & Accessories**: Absolute margin floor is 35.0%. Recommended target margin range is 50.0% to 75.0%.

## 3. Price Adjustment Cadence & Volatility Limits
To preserve channel partner trust and prevent customer churn, the pricing engine enforces adjustment limits:
- **Maximum Single-Step Price Increase**: No automated recommendation may exceed +15.0% in a single adjustment cycle.
- **Maximum Single-Step Price Decrease**: No automated markdown recommendation may exceed -20.0% in a single cycle.
- **Cooling-Off Period**: A minimum of 7 calendar days must elapse between consecutive price changes for the same SKU, unless flagged for emergency inventory clearance.
- **Competitor Benchmark Spread**: Recommended prices should generally remain within +/-15.0% of the median direct competitor price, unless the SKU demonstrates extreme inelasticity (|E_d| < 0.40) or distinct proprietary features.

## 4. Human Approval Authorization Matrix
Price changes are classified into tiers based on projected financial impact and percentage deviation:
- **Tier 1 (Automated Execution)**: Price adjustments within +/-3.0% and margin > 35% can be auto-executed under low-risk policies.
- **Tier 2 (Category Manager Approval)**: Price adjustments between +/-3.1% and +/-10.0%, or projected monthly revenue impact under $50,000, require Category Manager sign-off.
- **Tier 3 (VP / Pricing Committee Approval)**: Price adjustments exceeding +/-10.0%, margin reduction below 30.0%, or projected monthly revenue impact exceeding $50,000 require executive committee approval.

## 5. Cost Pass-Through & Inflation Governance
When supplier cost prices (C) increase:
- Inflationary cost adjustments must be evaluated within 48 hours of ERP cost updates.
- If demand elasticity is inelastic (|E_d| < 1.0), pass through 100% of cost increase plus standard markup.
- If demand elasticity is elastic (|E_d| > 1.0), pass through a minimum of 60% of the cost increase while optimizing volume via bundling or promotional financing.
